# Evaluation

Eligible submissions are evaluated by human judges.

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Technical Execution | 25% |
| Problem Significance | 20% |
| Evidence & Measurement | 20% |
| Real-World Usefulness | 20% |
| Open-Source Quality & Reproducibility | 15% |

## Technical Execution — 25%

Does the project work, and is the technical approach appropriate to the problem?

## Problem Significance — 20%

Is the problem clearly defined and meaningfully connected to secure, efficient or resilient systems?

## Evidence & Measurement — 20%

Does the submission provide credible evidence supporting the claims being made?

Teams are not expected to measure the same things. Evidence should match the project and the claim.

## Real-World Usefulness — 20%

Could the project plausibly be used, tested, extended or learned from beyond the hackathon?

## Open-Source Quality & Reproducibility — 15%

Can others understand, inspect and build upon the work?

## What does not automatically increase a score

Projects do not receive extra credit solely for:

- using AI
- using a more complex stack
- having a larger team
- producing a polished pitch video
- using expensive infrastructure

A focused project with strong execution and credible evidence can outperform a larger project that is difficult to validate.
