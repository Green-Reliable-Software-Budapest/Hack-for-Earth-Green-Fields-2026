# Code of Conduct

Green Reliable Software Budapest is committed to a professional, inclusive and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

Participants and contributors should:

- treat others with respect
- welcome different levels of experience
- critique ideas and code rather than people
- give credit for others' work
- respect privacy and consent
- avoid harassment, intimidation and discriminatory behaviour
- communicate technical disagreement in good faith

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, deliberate humiliation, discriminatory conduct, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, sustained disruption and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository activity, Hack for Earth community spaces and organiser-led programme interactions.

## Reporting

If an issue can be discussed safely in public, open a repository issue.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the organisers through an official Green Reliable Software Budapest or Hack for Earth community channel rather than posting identifying details publicly.

Organisers may remove content or restrict participation where necessary to protect the community.
